15 Student Discounts Nobody Tells You About in 2026

Updated for 2026 · Free first, cheap second

Being a student is expensive — until you realize your school email is basically a coupon. These are the discounts that actually save real money. Most just need a school email or a verification service like UNiDAYS or Student Beans.

What you get: A long free trial, then Prime at roughly half price.

Needs: School email.

Sign up for UNiDAYS and Student Beans first — they unlock most of this list. Then grab Prime Student and Notion Education. And never buy a laptop without checking the education store first.
