About PocketPicks

PocketPicks is a small, independent site for students and young adults. We find the free and budget-friendly apps, tools, and gear that genuinely help — and skip the hype.

We don't accept payment to rank things, and we only write about products we've used or carefully researched. Some links are affiliate links, which help keep the site running at no cost to you.
