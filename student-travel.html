10 Ways Students Can Travel for Less in 2026

Updated for 2026 · Free first, cheap second

Travel doesn't have to mean draining your savings. These ten tricks — most of them free to use — are how students and young travelers see the world on a part-time job's budget. The pattern is the same every time: plan loose, book early, and let the apps do the hunting.

1. Get an ISIC Student Card (Discounts worldwide)

Best for: Unlocking student discounts on museums, transport, and hostels in 130+ countries.

Watch out: Some discounts need the physical card, so order before you fly.

Price: One-time fee, pays for itself fast.

Get an ISIC card →

2. Skyscanner (Free flight search)

Best for: "Everywhere" searches and the cheapest-month view when your dates are flexible.

Not great for: Booking — use it to compare, then book direct.

Price: Free.

Search Skyscanner →

3. Hostelworld (Budget stays)

Best for: Cheap dorm beds and meeting other young travelers; reviews are reliable.

Watch out: Dorms aren't for everyone — filter by rating.

Price: Free to browse; you pay the hostel.

Book on Hostelworld →

4. Rome2Rio (Free route planner)

Best for: Figuring out the cheapest way to get from A to B across trains, buses, ferries, and flights.

Watch out: Prices are estimates — confirm before you commit.

Price: Free.

Plan with Rome2Rio →

5. FlixBus / Omio (Cheap ground travel)

Best for: Intercity buses and trains across Europe and beyond, often cheaper than flying.

Not great for: Speed — buses are slow but scenic.

Price: Free to use; tickets from a few euros.

Compare on Omio →

6. Couchsurfing (Free stays with locals)

Best for: Free places to sleep and local tips from hosts who know the city.

Watch out: Read references carefully and trust your gut on safety.

Price: Free (some verification fees).

Try Couchsurfing →

7. Google Maps Offline (Free, saves data)

Best for: Downloading city maps so you're not burning roaming data getting lost.

Watch out: Offline maps don't show live transit times.

Price: Free.

Use Google Maps →

8. StudentUniverse (Student-only fares)

Best for: Flights, hotels, and tours with fares reserved just for students and under-26s.

Watch out: You'll verify student status; deals vary by route.

Price: Free to search.

Find student fares →

9. Pack a reusable water bottle & utensils

Best for: Skipping paid bottles and takeout markup every single day abroad.

Watch out: Refill stations aren't everywhere — plan refills.

Price: One cheap bottle, endless savings.

Shop a travel bottle →

10. Travel off-season & mid-week

Best for: The single biggest saving — flights and hostels can drop 30–50% outside holidays.

Not great for: When you must travel during breaks.

Price: Free strategy, just shift your dates.

See flexible-date deals →

Our pick

Start with Skyscanner for flights and Hostelworld for beds, add an ISIC card for discounts, and travel mid-week. Do those four and you've already beaten most people's travel budgets.
